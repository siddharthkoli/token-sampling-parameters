From model probabilities to candidate set

Running total adds up the temperature-adjusted probabilities from the top. The purple line sits under the token where it first reaches p. The green line sits under the k-th token. Final chance is each survivor’s share after the cut tokens are removed.

The draw

Surviving tokens sit end to end, each slice as wide as its final chance. A random point lands somewhere on the strip.
